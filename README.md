This typically takes 30–90 days depending on the store. Your referral bonus unlocks once the qualifying purchase reaches Confirmed status.

You can share it in general communities, but posting it on any ShopBack merchant's Facebook page or forum is prohibited and will disqualify you. The spirit of the program is friend-to-friend sharing, not mass link distribution.
